## 2. Architecture principles

1. **Modules.** Every feature area is a module that can be switched on or off in Settings. Modules plug into shared services:
   - links;
   - universal search;
   - quick capture;
   - widgets;
   - App Intents;
   - encryption.
2. **Apple owns what Apple already stores.** Tasks live in **Apple Reminders**, events in **Apple Calendar** (EventKit), health data in **Apple Health** (HealthKit), and people in **Contacts**. The app reads and writes them there and never copies them into its own store. Apple syncs them for free.
3. **Notes are plain Markdown files** in real folders inside my iCloud Drive app folder. They stay readable by any other app.
4. **Everything else** lives in SwiftData (local) and syncs through per-device change logs in the iCloud Drive folder (section 6).
5. **Sensitive modules are encrypted:** Journal, Medical, Documents vault and People notes, using CryptoKit (section 7).
6. **Everything links to everything.** A shared `Link` model connects any two items, e.g. habit ↔ goal ↔ countdown ↔ note ↔ wish ↔ task. Every item has a stable `ItemRef` (`kind` + `id`) used by links, search, quick capture and deep links (`dissolve://item/<kind>/<id>`).
7. **On-device intelligence only.** Use Apple's Foundation Models framework and on-device speech recognition. Never send my data to a cloud AI. Hide AI features gracefully on devices without Apple Intelligence.

## 5. Modules (requirements)

### 5.1 Habits
- **Types:**
  - binary;
  - quantity (target + unit; partial progress counts);
  - timed (minutes; works with the focus timer);
  - limit (success means staying under).
- **Schedules:** daily · specific weekdays · X times per week · every N days.
- **Day status:** completed · partial · skipped (neutral) · missed · not scheduled.
- **Streak:** consecutive scheduled days completed. Skipped days neither break nor extend it. Weekly habits count streaks in weeks. An "X per week" habit doesn't break while the weekly target is still reachable.
- **Strength score:** Loop-style exponential smoothing, shown next to the streak.
- **Dates:**
  - Logs are stored by local calendar date (`yyyy-MM-dd`).
  - A "day ends at" setting (default 4 am) decides which day a late log counts for.
  - Past days are editable.
  - Schedule versions take effect from a date, so history isn't recalculated.
  - Archive keeps history.
- **Routines:** an ordered chain of habits (e.g. a morning routine) with a "Start routine" player that steps through them one by one.
- **Earned rest days:** for example, 1 skip earned for every 14 completed days (configurable). Using one keeps the streak.
- **"Never miss twice":** after a miss, the next day gets a gentle priority reminder.
- **Auto-completion sources:** Apple Health (steps, workouts, sleep, mindful minutes), LeetCode, GitHub, the focus timer, and location arrival.
- **Awards:** badges for streak milestones (7, 30, 100, 365), perfect weeks and months, and first-ever completions. Each award has a Fitness-style animated unveil and an awards shelf.

### 5.2 Consistency grid (signature feature — make it beautiful)
- **Per-habit grid:** a GitHub-style year view, 53 columns × 7 rows, with today at the right edge, month labels and a Less–More legend.
- **Overall grid:** the same layout, shaded by the share of scheduled habits done that day. Toggles also count "journaled today" and check-ins.
- **Levels:**

  | Level | Goal completion | Colour |
  |---|---|---|
  | 0 | none | system fill |
  | 1 | 1–33% | habit colour at 30% opacity |
  | 2 | 34–66% | habit colour at 50% opacity |
  | 3 | 67–99% | habit colour at 75% opacity |
  | 4 | 100%+ | habit colour at 100% opacity |

  Skipped days show as an outlined cell.
- **Interaction:** tap or hover shows a popover; tap again to edit that day.
- Build it from custom SwiftUI cells, not Swift Charts.

### 5.3 Countdowns
- **Fields:** title, symbol, colour, optional photo, date or date-and-time.
- **Display:** days · weeks + days · live D:H:M:S, plus a progress ring measured from the creation date.
- **Modes:** count-up ("days since"); yearly repeats (birthdays).
- **Arrival:** a celebration, then auto-archive.
- Can be linked to a goal or wish, e.g. "Marathon in 41 days — training 82% on track", with habit completion shown inside the countdown.

### 5.4 Life graph
- **Inputs:** date of birth and lifespan (default 90).
- **Layout:** "Life in Weeks", 90 × 52 = 4,680 cells, drawn with `Canvas`.
  - Past weeks filled, current week pulsing, future weeks outlined.
  - Each row starts on a birthday.
- **Headline:** e.g. "1,612 weeks lived · 3,068 remain (65.6%)".
- **Life eras** appear as coloured bands.
- **Milestones** pin life events to weeks, including fulfilled wishes and finished goals. Tapping a pin opens the item.
- **Overlay toggle:** tint past weeks by that week's consistency.

### 5.5 Journal (encrypted)
- **Templates** are ordered blocks: `heading | prompt | text | checklist | image | mood | rating | habitSummary | healthSummary`. Templates can include images.
- **Entries:**
  - A day's entry *copies* the template blocks; later template edits never change old entries.
  - One entry per day.
  - Each entry has mood (1–5), word count, and a link to its template.
  - There can be a template per weekday.
- **Images:**
  - `PhotosPicker` with multi-select; drag-and-drop and paste on Mac.
  - Resize to about 2,048 px on the long edge; save as HEIC plus a thumbnail.
- **Voice journaling:** dictate into any text block with on-device transcription (Speech framework).
- **Browsing:** "On this day", search, and filters by mood.
- **AI (on-device):** suggested prompts for tomorrow, auto mood tag suggestions, and a weekly summary.

### 5.6 Notes (Bear-like, folder-based)
- **Storage:**
  - Each note is a `.md` file in `Notes/<folder path>/` inside the iCloud Drive app folder.
  - Its images sit in `Notes/<folder>/assets/`.
  - Real nested folders. **No tags.**
- **Organisation:** pinned notes; sort by edited / created / title; drag-and-drop between folders; move, rename and trash (with restore).
- **Editor:**
  - Live Markdown with TextKit 2 wrapped for SwiftUI (`UITextView` / `NSTextView`). Formatting renders as I type, and the `#`, `**` and `_` markers fade.
  - Supports headings, bold / italic, checklists, quotes, code blocks, tables, inline images and links.
  - Beautiful typography and a focus mode.
- **Links:** `[[Note title]]` links between notes, plus backlinks. `[[...]]` can also link to any app item via `ItemRef`.
- **Actions:** "Turn line into → task / habit / countdown / flashcard".
- **Search:** fast full-text search through an index kept in SwiftData.
- **Mac layout:** three columns (folders → notes → editor). On iPhone, folders drill down into notes.
- **Conflicts:** if the same note was edited on two devices, detect it with `NSFileVersion` and offer "Keep this / Keep other / Keep both".

## 6. Data storage and sync (no CloudKit)

- **Shared folder setup:**
  - On first launch, each device picks the same folder in my iCloud Drive (iOS: document picker for folders; macOS: `NSOpenPanel`).
  - Save a security-scoped bookmark.
  - macOS needs App Sandbox plus `com.apple.security.files.user-selected.read-write`. Don't add an iCloud entitlement.
- **Folder layout:**
  ```
  Dissolve/
    changes/<deviceID>/<yyyy-MM>.jsonl     structured data change logs (sensitive records encrypted)
    snapshots/<deviceID>.jsonl             monthly compacted snapshots
    photos/<uuid>.heic + <uuid>_thumb.heic
    vault/<uuid>.enc                       encrypted documents
    Notes/<folders>/<note>.md + assets/    plain Markdown notes
    readlater/<uuid>/                      offline article copies
  ```
- **Change logs:**
  - Each device writes only its own log files.
  - Each line is `{entity, id, updatedAt, deleted, fields}`.
  - Merge on launch, on returning to the foreground, and in a `BGAppRefreshTask`.
  - Per record, the newest `updatedAt` wins; deletes are tombstones.
  - Reload widgets after merging.
- **Photos and vault files** are written once and never modified.
- **Notes:**
  - The files are the source of truth.
  - Watch for changes with `NSFilePresenter` / a periodic scan, and keep a SwiftData index for search and backlinks.
  - Handle conflicts with `NSFileVersion`.
- **Files:** use `NSFileCoordinator` for every read and write, and `startDownloadingUbiquitousItem(at:)` for files iCloud has offloaded.
- **Apple-owned data** (Reminders, Calendar, Health, Contacts) is never copied. Store only link metadata keyed by Apple's identifiers.
- **Tests:** test merge logic thoroughly in `Engine`.
- **If folder access fails in M0:** tell me. The options are a Supabase free project (500 MB database, 1 GB files, pauses after a week idle) or device-only storage with manual export/import.
- **Export everything:** a zip of JSON + Markdown notes and journal + images + decrypted vault (only after Face ID), via `fileExporter`. Also a monthly automatic export reminder.

## 7. Security and privacy

- **App lock:** Face ID / Touch ID **on every app launch** (decided). Journal, Medical, Vault and People notes also re-lock on their own.
- Blur the app content in the app switcher.
- **Encryption:** sensitive records and files use CryptoKit `AES.GCM` with a key derived from a passphrase I set once (HKDF / PBKDF2 with a stored salt). The key is stored in each device's local Keychain, protected by biometrics. No iCloud Keychain entitlement is needed.
- If I forget the passphrase, encrypted data is unrecoverable. Say so clearly during setup, and offer a printable recovery key.
- Widgets for sensitive modules show only counts or redacted content (`.privacySensitive()`).
- No analytics or third-party SDKs. The only network calls are Open-Meteo, LeetCode, GitHub and fetching read-later pages.

## 10. Visual design — it must feel like an Apple app

- **Liquid Glass:**
  - Standard containers get it automatically: `TabView`, `NavigationSplitView`, toolbars and sheets.
  - Add custom `.glassEffect()` only to a few floating controls: quick capture, "Journal today", grid range picker, editor formatting bars and the focus timer controls.
  - Group them in a `GlassEffectContainer` and morph with `glassEffectID`.
  - Content cards are **solid**: `.background(.background.secondary, in: .rect(cornerRadius: 22))`.
- **Motion:**

  | Effect | API | Where |
  |---|---|---|
  | Natural motion | `.snappy` (taps), `.smooth` (layout) | Every state change; never linear |
  | Rolling numbers | `.contentTransition(.numericText(value:))` | All changing numbers |
  | Haptics | `.sensoryFeedback` | `.success` on completion; `.increase` on +1 |
  | Living symbols | SF Symbols Draw On / Variable Draw / `.symbolEffect(.bounce)` | Checkmarks, filling quantity symbols |
  | Celebrations and award unveils | `PhaseAnimator` / `KeyframeAnimator` | Under 1.2 s |
  | Hero transitions | `.navigationTransition(.zoom)` + `.matchedTransitionSource` | Card → detail |
  | Shared elements | `matchedGeometryEffect` | Grid cell → popover |
  | Scroll polish | `.scrollTransition` | Card lists |
  | Living backgrounds | `MeshGradient` | Countdown, Life, Wishes and Today headers |

  Grid columns fill left to right on appear, and today pulses gently. Respect Reduce Motion by using crossfades.
- **Colour:** system colours only. App accent: green. Each module has its own system colour for its icon and tile, and each habit picks from ~12 colours.
- **Type:**
  - text styles only (Dynamic Type);
  - numbers use `.fontDesign(.rounded)` + `.monospacedDigit()`;
  - the notes editor uses a carefully tuned reading size and line height (like Bear).
- **Spacing:** 4-pt grid; margins of 16 pt (iPhone) and 20 pt (Mac); card padding 20; continuous corner radius 20–24.
- **Library tiles** look like app icons: rounded squares in the module colour with an SF Symbol.
- **Accessibility:** VoiceOver labels everywhere, including every grid cell (e.g. "March 3, 2 of 3 habits").
- **App icon:** I'll make it in Icon Composer — tell me how in M14. Idea: a glass tile grid with one lit square.

## 12. Data models (starting point — refine as needed)

  - `Routine` → ordered habit IDs
  - `Award`: kind, habitID, earnedAt
  - `RestDayBalance`

  - `JournalTemplate` → `[TemplateBlock]`

  - `Photo` (fileName, thumbFileName, width, height, caption)
- **Notes:** `NoteIndex` (path, title, folder, excerpt, linksOut, updatedAt). The file is the source of truth.
- `Link`: from ItemRef, to ItemRef. `TaskLink` / `EventLink`: Apple identifier ↔ ItemRef.
- **Goals:** `Goal` (title, area, period, parentID, status); `KeyResult` (target, current, unit)
- `CheckIn`: mood, energy, note, at
- **Medical:** `Medication`, `DoseSchedule`, `DoseLog`, `HealthProfile`, `DoctorVisit`, `LabReport`, `LabValue` (name, value, unit, refLow, refHigh, date), `Appointment`, `Vaccination`, `InsurancePolicy`, `Symptom`
- `Wish`: title, collection, why, costINR, targetDate, priority, status, coverPhotoID, links, location (lat/lon), savedINR, fulfilledAt
- **Money:** `Expense`, `Category`, `Budget`, `Subscription`
- **Learning:** `Book` / `Course`; `Deck`, `Card`, `ReviewLog`; `ReadLaterItem`
- **People:** `PersonLink` (contactIdentifier, frequencyDays, lastContactedAt, notes); `ImportantDate`
- `VaultDocument`: type, number, issueDate, expiryDate, fileID, ocrText (encrypted)
- **Lists:** `ListCollection`, `ListItem`
- **Focus:** `FocusSession`
- `IntegrationAccount`: kind, username (token in Keychain)
